# The Hedgehog and the Fox

“The fox knows many things, but the hedgehog knows one big thing.” A woodland minisite on Archilochus’s line and the thinkers who took it up: Isaiah Berlin, Philip Tetlock, Jim Collins, Nate Silver, Ronald Dworkin, Stephen Jay Gould and John Lewis Gaddis. Toys: fox tricks against a hedgehog, Berlin’s sorting game, a forecasting race, Collins’s three circles, flip cards, and a quiz that counts how many things you know and says whether you are a fox or a hedgehog.

**Live:** https://nanobotco.github.io/hedgehog-fox/

- `docs/index.html` — the whole site, one page, drawings made in code.
- `tools/card.py` — the share card, `docs/card.jpg`, drawn with the page’s own fox and hedgehog.
